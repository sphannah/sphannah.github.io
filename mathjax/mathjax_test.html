Key Point #1: Arbitrage relationship

\[k_{e} = r + \rho = \frac{D_{0}(1 + g)}{P_{0}} + \frac{P_{1} - P_{0}}{P_{0}} = \frac{D_{1}}{P_{0}} + \frac{P_{1} - P_{0}}{P_{0}} = \omega\]

Required rate of return on equities = risk-free rate + risk premium = expected return on equities = dividend yield plus % expected capital gain = rate of return on capital

Key Point #2: Linking growth of financial variables with investment

\[g = \omega(1 - \phi) = k_{e}(1 - \phi)\]

In equilibrium, the growth of earnings, dividends and share prices are driven by the productivity of capital and the degree to which earnings are re-invested in the business

Key Point #3: Pricing equities (derived directly from #1 and #2)

\[k_{e} = \frac{D_{1}}{P_{0}} + g \Rightarrow \frac{D_{1}}{P_{0}} = k_{e} - g \Rightarrow\]

\[P_{0} = \frac{D_{1}}{k_{e} - g} = \frac{D_{1}}{r + \rho - g} = D_{0}\sum_{t = 1}^{\infty}\left\lbrack \frac{1 + g}{1 + k_{e}} \right\rbrack^{t}\]

Equity price is the present value of the future dividend stream (assumed infinite and growing at a constant pace) using the required rate of return on equities as the discount rate

Key Point #4: Interpreting Forward PE (use the definition, #2 and #3)

\[\frac{P_{0}}{E_{1}} = \frac{{\phi P}_{0}}{D_{1}} = \frac{\phi}{\frac{D_{1}}{P_{0}}} = \frac{\phi}{k_{e} - g} = \frac{\phi}{\phi k_{e}} = \frac{1}{k_{e}}\]

The forward earnings yield equals the required rate of return on equities
